UNIVERSITY PROFILE

Founded by the 7th Nizam.
Shaping Minds for Over a Century.

Established through a firman issued on 26 April 1917 and beginning operations in 1918, Osmania University was India's first to adopt Urdu as its medium of instruction. Spread across a sprawling 1,632-acre campus in eastern Hyderabad, it today spans 12 faculties, 53 departments, and 66 disciplines — serving approximately 4 lakh students across 739 affiliated colleges in 6 districts of Telangana. Re-accredited NAAC A+ Grade, and ranked in the QS World Rankings 2026. Its University College of Engineering, established in 1929, was the 6th engineering college in all of British India.

65+ Years of ECE Excellence

From two faculty members in 1959 to a powerhouse of research, innovation and industry collaboration — shaping engineers for over six decades.

DEPARTMENT ORIGIN

Founded by a Visionary Professor.
Built for Generations of Engineers.

The Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering was established in 1959 at UCE, Osmania University — with just two faculty members and an unshakeable vision. It bears the indelible stamp of its founder and first Head of Department, Late Prof. K. Krishnan Nair, who exhorted the department to become a byname for Excellence, Creativity and Enterprise. Over six decades, it has grown into a hub of 16 teaching faculty, 500+ students, and specialisations spanning Digital Systems, Signal Processing, VLSI, Microwaves, Embedded Systems, and Communications Engineering.
